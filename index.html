05 / GOOD TO KNOW

The finer
details.

A few useful answers before we talk.

Is this built for a particular kind of legal practice?+

Chamber Management System is designed for legal chambers that need a shared case diary, journal records, follow-up tasks and hearing-history management. We can discuss how it fits your workflow.

Can multiple chamber members use the system?+

Yes. The application supports administrator, staff and read-only viewer roles for authorized users. Access to the actual chamber portal is separate from this public website.

What happens when the next court date is not given yet?+

A journal entry can record that the next hearing date will be provided later, instead of inventing a scheduled date. The case can be updated when the new date becomes available.

Can I see tasks and reminders?+

Yes. Tasks can be associated with cases and journal records, with due dates and in-app reminder timing. This version of the product does not promise email or mobile push notifications.

Does the system work on a phone?+

The chamber portal has a responsive interface intended for modern desktop and mobile browsers. Ask for a walkthrough to discuss your team's devices and requirements.

How do I request more information?+

Use any “Get in touch” or “Request a walkthrough” link on this page to open an email addressed to madushangarodrigo@gmail.com.
