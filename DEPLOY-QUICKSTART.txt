CHAMBER MANAGEMENT SYSTEM — PUBLIC MARKETING WEBSITE

1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Cloudflare Dashboard → Workers & Pages → Create → Pages → Direct Upload.
3. Upload the EXTRACTED CONTENTS of the site folder (index.html at the root).
4. Open your new public .pages.dev URL.
5. Test the four clickable tabs in the product preview and the email inquiry button.

IMPORTANT: Deploy as a NEW public Cloudflare Pages project, NOT to your existing private CMS Worker. No database migrations, D1 setup or member logins are needed.
